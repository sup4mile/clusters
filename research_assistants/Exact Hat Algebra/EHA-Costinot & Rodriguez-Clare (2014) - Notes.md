# Notes on Costinot & Rodriguez-Clare, "Trade Theory with Numbers", Handbook of International Economics, Volume 4 (2014)
Section 2 introduces an Armington trade model with a single product endowment per country, CES preferences, and no intermediates.

Section 2.1. presents the details of the model.

Section 2.2. shows how changes in welfare in country $j$ (i.e., real consumption $\hat{C}_j$) can be computed based on changes in macroeconomic observables (expenditure shares, income levels) for a given trade elasticity (denoted $\varepsilon$).

Section 2.3. illustrates the procedure for a counterfactual experiment where countries retreat to autarky. The procedure exploits the fact $\lambda_{jj}=1$ (i.e., the expenditure share of the domestic good is unity) and therefore the gross change in the expenditure share is given by $\hat{\lambda}_{jj} = \tfrac{1}{\lambda_{jj}}$, where $\lambda_{jj}$ is the initial expenditure share on the domestic good.
